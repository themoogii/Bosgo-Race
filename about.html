ABOUT

Bosgo is an elimination-style night race where every runner begins with a 10K qualifying stage, and only the fastest advance to the final 3K. Built around the belief that limits are meant to be challenged, Bosgo combines racing, atmosphere, and community into one unforgettable experience. Join us on October 3 and discover what lies beyond.

7k Club

7k Club is the running community behind Bosgo: a group built around discipline, shared miles, hard mornings and the quiet standard of showing up. The club keeps the race alive before and after the start line through training, stories, crews and digital memories from the road.
